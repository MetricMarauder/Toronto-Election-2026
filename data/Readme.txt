Notice:
-In reviewing the results, the Poll by Poll information has been corrected for two subdivisions: ward 21 subdivision 11 and 14
-It was determined that the tabulators assigned to these two subdivisions were inadvertently delivered to each other's voting locations. Both tabulators remained within the correct ward.
-The tabulators accurately counted all votes. However, because they were used in the opposite subdivisions, the vote totals were reported under the wrong subdivision on election night. 
-The tabulator delivery error had no impact on the official results or the vote count.
